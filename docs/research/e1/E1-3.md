# E1-3: CARS-v1 implementation and frozen ablations

Four new strategies and seven-policy comparisons are implemented. All policies complete the same finite cohort at each load. The known-task and historical-pressure modules improve over EDF+Carbon in the congested cases, but **Full does not consistently outperform RiskOnly**. This is a descriptive first-version model experiment, not a proof of novelty, optimality, calibrated risk, or real-cluster deployment benefit.

## Implementation

The unchanged three-policy registry remains intact. New policies use [cars.py](../../../experiments/e1/cars.py), immutable online state/history in [cars_state.py](../../../experiments/e1/cars_state.py), and [cars_feasibility.py](../../../experiments/e1/cars_feasibility.py). [engine.py](../../../experiments/e1/engine.py) adds four optional read-only notifications; all seven policies retain the public executor's three-resource admission, backfilling, nonpreemptive execution, timing and unchanged IT/HVAC measurement. The independent driver is [cars_experiment.py](../../../experiments/e1/cars_experiment.py), with [frozen parameters](../../../experiments/e1/configs/e1_3_cars.json), [analysis/figure source](../../../experiments/e1/analyze_cars.py), [method](E1-3-method.md) and [pseudocode](E1-3-algorithm-pseudocode.md).

EDF+Carbon controls for sorting. KnownOnly compares NOW versus DEFER for all current pending tasks (cap256) and fixed running holds over eight decisions/two hours, using identical EDF/backfilling. Additional missed IDs prohibit defer; any unknown or cap overflow executes conservatively. This checks a specific bounded forward schedule, not global feasibility. Full and RiskOnly additionally use observed arrivals in (t−120min,t], resource×rounded occupied duration / (capacity×observed window), max across CPU/GPU/MEM. Fixed 0/1/2-step buffers at rho0.5/1.0 preserve slack without changing capacity. At initial zero-length history rho=null, buffer2; no fabricated prehistory or future trace access. Current starts and issued deferrals update subsequent same-step checks.

Chronus motivates deadline/resource checks, but its MILP and preemptible leases are not transplanted ([primary paper §3.3](https://yezhisheng.com.cn/publication/chronus/chronus_preprint.pdf)). AFS motivates conservative future resource assumptions; this work ratio is our separate heuristic, not elastic GPU sharing ([primary paper §2.2/3.1](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/nsdi21-hwang.pdf)). GREEN motivates capacity-constrained carbon/JCT tradeoffs and explicitly does not ensure individual deadline SLOs ([primary paper §3/6.3](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/nsdi25-xu-kaiqiang.pdf)). These relationships do not establish novelty.

## Frozen design and accounting

Exact E1-2B cohorts are imported, not regenerated: 9943/12428/14914/19886 tasks, nested deterministic simultaneous clones, seed20261010. This is synthetic pressure. US-CAL-CISO; CPU50000/GPU1000/MEM80000; unchanged resource multiplier5/SLA1.5; 15min. Arrival interval [2023-07-01 05:00,2023-07-03 05:00) UTC, actual CI/weather through Drain. Fixed threshold137.65gCO2e/kWh is the preceding nonoverlapping June calibration median; original binary representation is retained. No policy sees future CI or arrivals. Parameters were declared before pilot and not tuned to observed results.

Every policy completes all tasks, with no final pending/running/censoring. At each load resource-hours agree and the common endpoint is 2023-07-03 13:45 UTC (56.75h); earlier drains integrate the same persistent idle physical state. Resource-hours are grid-held occupation, not exact-duration continuous work. Electric energy is IT+HVAC kWh; carbon is kWh×gCO2e/kWh/1000 kgCO2e. Transmission energy is zero and is never used as total energy. The CSV contains IT/HVAC/total energy, CPU/GPU/MEM average/peak use, queues, policy/capacity task-interval counts, own drain, JCT, waiting, completion and censoring. Local archives additionally retain fixed/own/common phases separately.

## Limits and stopping point

Aggregate/fractional resources, synthetic deadlines, fixed duration, grid release, nonpreemptive tasks, no migration/elasticity, one DC, noncontemporaneous workload/CI mapping and simultaneous clones. Finite-cohort Drain differs from long-running steady arrival. One window/seed supports descriptive paired comparisons, not significance, universal superiority or deployment claims. All arrivals and all declared variants are retained; no hidden favorable selection, post-result threshold changes or single-event causality claim. The unstable Full/Risk interaction and conservative fallback frequency need review before further tuning. This stage stops here; no RL, multi-DC or elastic GPU development.
